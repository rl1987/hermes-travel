# Client intake (do this before any search)

Advisors fail when they jump to hotels. Lock a structured brief first.

## Hard constraints (ask until you have them)

- Purpose of the trip (celebration, rest, explore, work, family)
- Dates and how flexible they are
- Who is traveling (count, ages, rooming, mobility, diet)
- Budget ceiling and what it must include (stay only vs stay+transport+food)
- Home airport or origin city
- Must-do and must-avoid
- Loyalty programs, cabin class, lodging style (hotel / home / hostel / mix)

If something is missing, ask. Do not invent passport, visa, or budget facts.

## Soft preferences

Pace (slow vs packed), neighborhoods they like, whether they want a highlight at the end of the trip, jet-lag recovery on night one.

## Output

Write a brief the rest of the plan can reuse:

```
Travelers:
Origin:
Dates:
Budget:
Pace:
Non-negotiables:
Avoid:
Lodging style:
Open questions:
```

Then stop and confirm the brief with the traveler before routing or stays.
