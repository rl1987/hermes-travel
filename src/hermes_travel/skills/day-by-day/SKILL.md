# Day-by-day build

Each day is a story, not a supplier list. Same shape every day so it is scannable.

## Template

- **Theme / zone** — one neighborhood or town
- **Morning** — highest-energy or timed-entry item
- **Afternoon** — nearby, slower, or transit
- **Evening** — meal or rest near the stay
- **Open block** — 40% of waking hours unscheduled
- **Moves** — how they get between items and how long it really takes (add buffer to map estimates)
- **Stay** — property name, check-in/out only on change days
- **Plan B** — weather or closure backup for the anchor

## Rules

- 60/40: plan 60% of the day, leave 40% open. Over-scheduling is why itineraries die on day two.
- No peak activity right after a long transfer.
- Museums often close Monday — flag unknown opening hours instead of guessing.
- Pause-and-verify: show Day 1, get a nod, then Day 2. Do not dump a 14-day brick unreviewed.

## Voice

“Wake up in Trastevere, walk to the morning slot at the Borghese, lunch in the same zone” — not “Hotel X, Tour Y, Lunch TBD.”
Name the property and the why for headline stays.
