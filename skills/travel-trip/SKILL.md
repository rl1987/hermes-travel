# Trip planning SOP

Work in modules. Do not collapse intake, research, days, and quoting into one dump.

1. **Intake** — load `travel-intake`. Confirm the brief.
2. **Architecture** — load `itinerary-architecture`. Live `plan_leg` and `compare_stays` (or lodging leaf tools). Anchors before filler.
3. **Days** — load `day-by-day`. Geographic clusters, 60/40 pace, pause after Day 1.
4. **Quote** — load `quote-and-docs`. Options with a pick. No fake bookings.

If the traveler changes dates or cities, return to architecture, do not patch a day in isolation.
