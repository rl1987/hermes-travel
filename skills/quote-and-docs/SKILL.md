# Quote, then documents (human books)

Statuses: researching → quoting → awaiting approval → ready to book. A draft itinerary is not a booking.

## Quote pack

For each service (stay, leg, transfer): dates, party, option A/B, price+currency, what is included, refundability, and a recommend/why. Totals plus 10–15% contingency. Split stay / transport / food / activities if the budget was given that way.

Do not invent confirmation numbers. Do not charge or hold inventory.

## After they approve

Remind them what a live advisor would book first: long-haul, then nights, then scarce experiences. Keep first and last night flexible if inbound can delay.

## Source of truth (client-facing)

One document:

- Day-by-day
- Property names and addresses
- Leg times and how to the hotel
- Open questions (visas, insurance, opening hours to re-check two weeks out)
- Emergency blanks (local emergency number, stay phone) — leave blank if unknown

Internal notes (assumptions, rejected options) stay off the client page.
